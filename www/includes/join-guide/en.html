Your adventure starts here.

A compatible game client, your server account and the connection instructions provided by the team.

01 Get the game client

Install the game version supported by this server. Check the server’s announcements or ask the team which version to use before downloading or updating.

02 Create your account

Register on this website. Use your server account’s email address and password to sign in to the game. Your character name and Blizzard account are not your server login.

Create an account

Choose a unique password. Never share it with other players or staff.

03 Prepare your connection

Follow the connection method published by this server’s team. The required settings and any optional tools depend on the server; ask the team if they have not been provided.

04 Join a realm

Launch your configured game client, sign in with your server account, then choose an available realm and create or select your character.

Check realm status

Having trouble?

Check that your client version is supported, your account details are correct and the realm is online. If you need help, send the team the exact error message—never your password.
