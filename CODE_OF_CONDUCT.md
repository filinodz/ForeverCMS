# Community code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, patient and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, never people.
- Respect privacy and never request or publish credentials or player data.
- Credit original work, sources and contributors accurately.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about scope, safety and project direction.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disruption, plagiarism
and sexualized or insulting conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when behavior is harmful. Report conduct concerns privately to the
repository owner through a private channel listed on the
[FilinoDZ GitHub profile](https://github.com/filinodz). Do not expose sensitive
details in a public issue.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the
project. Enforcement will consider context, impact and the safety of the community.
